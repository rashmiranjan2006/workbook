Chapter 1

Product overview

GenAxle is a two-sided, performance-driven marketplace and affiliate platform. Companies publish offers and receive verified leads. Earners share offers, submit customer leads and earn commission when deals close. Admins audit leads, verify payments and operate the platform.

MVP boundaries

  • Marketplace discovery by industry category, public and signed in.
  • Separate registration and onboarding for Companies, Earners, Campus Marketing Champions (CMC) and Influencers.
  • Lead pipeline: Lead Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Deal Closed, Commission Pending, Payment Sent, Commission Paid, or Rejected.
  • 48-hour approval window with live countdown clocks.
  • Two-sided payment verification: company proof of payment, earner proof of receipt, then admin approval.
  • Admin Private Room with a secondary key and immutable audit logs.
  • Socket.IO chat between companies and earners, tied to leads.

Key design decisions

  • Frontend is a static SPA; backend is a stateless REST API.
  • Dual-token auth: short-lived access token in memory, 7-day refresh token in an httpOnly cookie.
  • Identity (User) is separate from role profiles (Earner, Company, CMC, Influencer), linked by userId.
  • Private Room uses its own token (x-private-room-token) and server-side timeouts.
  • On company verification, the backend generates a PDF registration certificate with PDFKit and attaches the Business Partner Kit.
Chapter 2

System architecture

A decoupled client-server design: a React SPA on Vercel, a modular monolith Express API with Socket.IO, MongoDB Atlas for data, Cloudinary for logos and Resend (SMTP fallback) for email. The ChatWidget calls Groq directly from the browser.

Request lifecycle

Every call goes through the Axios client in src/lib/api.js, which attaches the bearer token and the private room token when present. On a 401 it calls /api/auth/refresh-token with the cookie and replays the request. If refresh fails, the user is sent to /login.

Authorization gates

Public routes pass straight through. Others run protect (JWT), then allowRoles, then resolveCompanyContext for companies. Admin Private Room routes add verifyPrivateRoom and write an AuditLog entry.

Errors and logging

Errors return { success:false, message, code, errors[] }. Validation failures return 400 with field-level details. Admin Private Room actions are persisted to the AuditLog collection; other logging goes to stdout.

Chapter 6

Backend

server.js connects to MongoDB, creates the HTTP server, attaches Socket.IO and listens on PORT (default 5000). app.js builds the middleware chain and mounts 20 routers.

Middleware order

helmet, cookieParser, cors, express.json, static uploads, routes with domain middleware, controller, global error handler. CORS reads CLIENT_ORIGIN (comma-separated), treats localhost and 127.0.0.1 as equivalent and allows requests with no origin.

Mounted routers

/api/auth, /api/admin/private (two routers), /api/admin (two routers), /api/dashboard, /api/offers, /api/leads, /api/analytics, /api/referrals, /api/withdrawals, /api/payment-verifications, /api/notifications, /api/conversations, /api/messages, /api/upload, /api/contact, /api/business-onboarding, /api/marketplace, /api/search-analytics.

Guards

  • protect: verifies bearer JWT, loads the user, rejects suspended or unverified accounts.
  • allowRoles(...): 403 on role mismatch.
  • optionalAuth: used for search analytics.
  • verifyPrivateRoom: checks the token signature, the PrivateRoomSession record and inactivity timeout.

Uploads, email and PDFs

Avatars and chat files go to local disk; company logos are buffered in memory and streamed to Cloudinary. Limits: 5 MB images, 10 MB documents; JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF only. Email uses Resend with a Nodemailer SMTP fallback. Company registration produces a PDF certificate attached alongside the Business Partner Kit.

Auto-expiry

expirePendingLeads in lead.service.js handles leads still in Lead Submitted after 48 hours, flagging or transitioning them for admin review.

Payment verification flow

  • Company closes the deal, pays the commission and uploads proof (UTR or receipt); lead moves to Commission Pending.
  • Earner confirms receipt and uploads a statement; status becomes UNDER_ADMIN_REVIEW.
  • Admin compares both proofs side by side and approves; lead becomes Commission Paid and the earner balance is credited.

Business rules

stateDiagram-v2
[*] --> LeadSubmitted
LeadSubmitted --> UnderReview
LeadSubmitted --> Expired: 48h
UnderReview --> Approved
UnderReview --> Rejected
Approved --> DealClosed
DealClosed --> CommissionPending
CommissionPending --> UnderAdminReview
UnderAdminReview --> CommissionPaid
Rejected --> Disputed
Disputed --> Approved
Disputed --> Rejected
  • BR-LD-01 Company must act within 48 hours of submission; after that the lead is eligible for reassignment or admin action.
  • BR-LD-02 Earners cannot edit customer phone or email after submission.
  • BR-LD-03 Companies see and update only leads for their own offers.
  • BR-LD-04 Percentage offers: commission = dealValue × rate ÷ 100. Fixed offers: commission = the fixed amount.

Accounts, payouts and referrals

  • Unverified login returns 403 EMAIL_NOT_VERIFIED; suspended users are blocked by protect; disposable emails are rejected.
  • Minimum withdrawal is ₹100. Requesting a payout moves the amount from available to pending balance; cancel or reject returns it. A UPI ID, or bank account plus IFSC, is required first.
  • Each earner gets a permanent code GEN-<suffix>. Signing up with a code records referredBy and creates a referral document. Influencer conversions are written to InfluencerLedger.
  • Private Room needs PRIVATE_ROOM_SECRET, expires after 30 minutes idle, and every sensitive action writes an AuditLog before it executes.

Security

Access15 min to 1 hJWT_ACCESS_SECRET

Email verification24 hJWT_EMAIL_SECRET

  • Passwords use bcrypt with 10 to 12 rounds and are hidden from queries by default.
  • Backend RBAC is authoritative; ProtectedRoute on the frontend is only a UX gate.
  • Private Room login is limited to 5 failed attempts per 15 minutes.
  • Helmet sets nosniff and frame protection; CORS is limited to CLIENT_ORIGIN with credentials.
  • express-validator checks every write endpoint; Zod validates forms in the browser.
  • Uploads are restricted by mime type and size; public logos go to Cloudinary.

Chapter 13

Deployment and operations

  • Frontend: Vercel, root frontend, build npm run build, output dist, Node 20 or 22. vercel.json rewrites all non-asset paths to /index.html.
  • Backend: Render or a VPS running node src/server.js on process.env.PORT. Use PM2, Docker or systemd for restarts.
  • CORS: set CLIENT_ORIGIN=https://www.genaxle.com,https://genaxle.com.
  • Health: poll GET /api/health every 60 seconds; expect {"success":true,"message":"GenAxle Backend Running"}.
  • Rollback: promote the previous Vercel deployment; redeploy the previous backend commit; enable Atlas daily snapshots with point-in-time recovery.

Pre-release manual checklist

  • Earner registers, verifies email, logs in, submits a lead and sees the countdown.
  • Company approves, closes the deal and uploads payment proof; lead is Commission Pending.
  • Earner uploads receipt; admin approves; balance increases and the lead is Commission Paid.
  • Admin enters the Private Room and finds the login audit record.

Data lifecycle and privacy

Credentials and tokensCriticalHidden by default, never sent to the frontend.

Data flows from Zod validation in the browser, over TLS, through express-validator, sanitization and hashing, into MongoDB, with audit records for admin actions. Private Room sessions expire by TTL index. Audit logs, leads and payment verifications are kept permanently for disputes and reconciliation.

Chapter 18

Assessment and roadmap

Known gaps

  • Mock features: company campaigns, creatives, reports, applications and admin campaigns have no backend (medium).
  • Test gaps: no automated tests for the lead lifecycle or payment verification (high); memory-server download timeouts (medium).
  • Chat uploads on local disk: lost on container restart or across instances (high).
  • Socket.IO single node: no Redis adapter, so no horizontal scaling (medium).
  • Hygiene: CRLF lint errors on Windows; Vite bundle warnings (index.js 2,018 kB, ui.js 596 kB); Google login unconfigured (low).
